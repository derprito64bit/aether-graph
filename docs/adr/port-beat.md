# ADR: port beat placement (Prompt B section 5.4)

Decision: extend `approach` with a rail traverse that ends on the bottom
edge (option 1).

Why: option 2 (a new beat between `storage` and `battery`) shifts every
act boundary after it and invalidates all thirteen film snapshots, the
overlay chapter copy, and the state windows in one stroke. The port needs
two seconds, not a renumbering. The approach arc already travels the rail;
ending it low on the USB-C mouth, tongue, and asymmetric grille gives the
receptacle its beat, and the 0.25–0.28 window swings back up into the
x-ray framing as a natural exhale.

Consequence: the 0.25 key drops its `fit` (fixed 24mm lens on the rail)
and tips the phone forward (rx 0.1) so the mouth faces the camera. The
x-ray entry absorbs the return swing; director damping and velocity caps
keep flicks controlled through it.
